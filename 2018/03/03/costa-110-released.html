New release of Costa - version 1.1.0

Posted March 3rd, 2018
 

Yesterday I released a new version of my long-time hobby coding project, Costa.

There’s not much new to see. I added a “Tip of the Day” dialog, and some new icons for you to choose from when creating desktop links, but mostly it’s all about bugfixes and improved stability. The changelog for version 1.1.0 can be found in the archive.

For those who are interested in development, visit Codeberg to have a look at the source code. It’s not the most fancy codebase as it has been coded on-and-off over the past decade, but it’s stable and fairly compact.
