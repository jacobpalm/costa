Costa 0.8.0 released

Posted July 30th, 2005
 

I’ve just released version 0.8.0 of my QuickBASIC GUI, Costa for MS-DOS. It has many new features, including themes and multiuser support. No accessories are included (didn’t wanna include half-finished tools), but it’s still a fully functional GUI.

You can download Costa here: https://costa.jacobpalm.dk/downloads/costa080.zip

Or, if you want more information about this beta, check the archive: https://costa.jacobpalm.dk/archive/080.html#versioninfo

Note: Before running Costa, be sure to read INFO!!!.TXT and run USERS.EXE (read the document first).

I’m open to suggestions and comments.
